## Most of it is the same text, read again

This is the correction that matters most, and the first version of this file got it wrong. A conversation resends its entire history on every turn, and those resends are billed as `cache_read` tokens. They are real tokens and real spend — but they are not new writing, and comparing them to books implies they are.

|---|---:|---:|
| re-read from cache | 11,455,211,249 |  95.5% |
| written to cache | 410,840,760 |   3.4% |
| sent fresh | 71,574,272 |   0.6% |
| **generated by the model** | 55,891,501 |   0.5% |

**538,306,533 tokens were new content** — everything else is the conversation being re-sent. On the machine this was written on, one chat of 2,613 turns cost 1,301,790,690 tokens, of which 3,089,179 (0.24%) was generated text.
