## Most of it is the same text, read again

This is the correction that matters most, and the first version of this file got it wrong. A conversation resends its entire history on every turn, and those resends are billed as `cache_read` tokens. They are real tokens and real spend — but they are not new writing, and comparing them to books implies they are.

| | tokens | share |
|---|---:|---:|
| re-read from cache | 46,434,102,588 |  94.7% |
| written to cache | 2,017,371,064 |   4.1% |
| sent fresh | 406,775,217 |   0.8% |
| **generated by the model** | 196,000,454 |   0.4% |

**2,620,146,735 tokens were new content** — everything else is the conversation being re-sent. On the machine this was written on, one chat of 2,613 turns cost 1,301,790,690 tokens, of which 3,089,179 (0.24%) was generated text.
