UNIT SN#53890 

We threw it into a tester. 
Ran a basic GPUCheck, for our last two GPUs, in the far left, was "ff", whereas all the other GPUs had "a1". This means the GPUs are bad. 
My initial thought was to take it out of the test and reseat but instead I ran a BBX. The BBX showed that there was a row remapping error, which 
could not be fixed by a reseat. We saved ourselves the trouble of reseating. Though, we knew the bus locations of our bad GPUs before hand. 

We brought the unit out of the tester and did a GPUswap with GoldenGPUs. (NOTE: When swapping GPUs in general. They must be from different clusters.)
